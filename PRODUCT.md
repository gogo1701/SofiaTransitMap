# Product

**Who:** Sofia residents and visitors who use public transport, plus anyone curious about how punctual the network is.
That includes journalists, civic groups and the municipality itself.

**Goals**
1. Answer "where is my bus, and when will it come?" in seconds.
2. Plan a door-to-door trip for now or for any future date and time, using the official timetable.
3. Make punctuality transparent with a growing history of delays by line, hour, weekday and mode.

**Tone:** calm, factual and civic. The interface is Bulgarian first with English available. It shows numbers before
adjectives and never relies on colour alone.

**Principles**
- Official open data only, from the GTFS feeds of the Urban Mobility Center, with attribution.
- Live data is clearly marked as live, and scheduled data is never presented as realtime.
- The interface is fast on a phone at a stop.
